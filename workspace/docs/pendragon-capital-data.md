# Pendragon Capital Management — Data Overview

## About
Pendragon Capital is a multi-strategy hedge fund operating four distinct sub-funds:

| Fund | Strategy | Focus |
|------|----------|-------|
| **Excalibur** | Core Long/Short | Flagship fund, broad market exposure |
| **Arthur Equity** | Fundamental Equity | Deep-value equity picks, healthcare & tech focus |
| **Macro Alpha** | Global Macro | Rates, FX, commodities, cross-asset |
| **Gawain Risk Parity** | Risk Parity | Volatility-balanced allocation |

## Data Access
